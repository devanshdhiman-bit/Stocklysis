ℹ️ About StockLysis

What is StockLysis?

StockLysis is an educational stock market platform built to help beginners and intermediate investors understand the Indian stock market better. From real-time-style dashboards to curated market news, watchlists, stock comparisons, and beginner tips — everything is designed to be simple, intuitive, and visually engaging.

Our goal is to demystify investing for the everyday person. Whether you're tracking your first stock or comparing big names like TCS vs INFY, StockLysis gives you the tools to make more informed decisions — without overwhelming jargon.
